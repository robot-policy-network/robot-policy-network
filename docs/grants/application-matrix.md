## Circle Developer Grants — verified parameters (2026-09-20)

- **Status:** open, rolling review, single application channel at `circle.com/grant`.
- **Relaunched:** 2026-05-14 with a "more selective, milestone-driven, Arc-forward" approach.
- **Six priority use cases (official):** **agentic economic activity** (listed first — our
  direct fit), stablecoin FX, peer-to-peer payments, treasury management, prediction
  markets, lending & borrowing.
- **Core requirement:** "Arc is core to your flow of value, liquidity, or settlement,"
  with Circle products (USDC, Wallets, CCTP, Gateway, Agent Stack, Nanopayments) as
  meaningful building blocks — not incidental.
- **Funding:** tiered USDC, milestone-based, released as milestones are met and
  approved. **Circle publishes no dollar amounts or ranges** — we propose a budget;
  Circle sizes the award.
- **Review:** 5 steps — Grants team alignment review → technical review with Circle
  Ventures / Product / BD → tailored milestone design → disbursement per milestone.
- **Reapplication:** allowed after final milestones are complete.

## Rules

- Never invent deadlines, amounts, or URLs. If a field says "Verify", it is
  unknown until an official page is checked and the check date is recorded.
- Before each submission, re-read the Canonical Fact Sheet and remove any
  claim that moved out of "Verified".
- Record confirmation numbers / submission receipts in the last column
  immediately after submitting.
